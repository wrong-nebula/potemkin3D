## 3. ~~Consistency: synchronised views + texture-space consolidation~~ (done: `src/core/consolidate.ts`, `src/core/texfusion.ts`, `src/core/jbu.ts`, `--consolidate`)

**Why:** sequential painting drifts. `--parallel` leaves visible seams. "Next is an automatic retouch pass for seams and odd patches."

- **~~Post-process first~~** (done: `src/core/consolidate.ts`, `--consolidate`, `trompe.consolidate()`, MCP `consolidate` step; zero model calls). Back-projects each view into atlas space with per-texel confidence `w = (lens × cosθ / d) × depthContinuity × rimFade`, solves a damped Lucas-Kanade surface-tangent flow field on high-pass structural luminance (invariant to parallel exposure/colour drift) and advects overlapping colours along it, then solves a 3-level multigrid Screened-Poisson stitch within each UV island / part boundary (`Atlas.updatePaint` / `Atlas.applyConsolidation`, preserving native 4K/8K detail, per-texel capture quality, `fidelity.ts` masks and downstream ground/part/finish passes).
  - **Measured (`--parallel 6`, 18 views, 0 extra passes):** on **Courtyard** (`16,305` boundary edges, 2.4 s), seam step drops **15.8 → 5.4 RGB (-66%)** and seam ratio drops **2.74 → 1.02**; on **Hall** (`31,163` boundary edges, 2.5 s), seam step drops **14.7 → 4.1 RGB (-72%)** and seam ratio drops **3.34 → 1.00** while retaining >91% of interior grain contrast.
- **MRF view-label assignment & bilateral soft back-projection (`solveMrfViewLabels`, `softBackprojectPool`, `backProjectView(..., { softKernel: true })`).** Replaces per-texel `argmax` winner selection with α-expansion / ICM Markov Random Field regularisation weighted by `-log(weight)` unary cost plus pairwise `(1 + RGB discrepancy)` boundary penalties so cuts follow mortar and low-discrepancy contours, paired with Im2SurfTex-style 3×3 tangent-plane depth/normal/confidence-weighted bilateral soft back-projection (`softKernel: true`) and geodesic atlas pooling (`softBackproject: true`).
  - **Measured:** MRF cuts noisy salt-and-pepper winner perimeter by **97% (`2,207 → 65` boundary edges)** and lowers raw pre-Poisson seam step **30.2 → 26.0 RGB**, while tangent-plane bilateral soft back-projection suppresses foreground silhouette colour bleed to `≤ 2 RGB` and `CONSOLIDATE_JBU_FRAG` (`src/core/jbu.ts`, adapted from `mrdoob/three.js#35602`) prevents 1K consolidation offsets from bleeding across 90° creases when written back to 4K/8K atlases.
- **Joint generation — Sequential Interlaced Multiview Sampler (`src/core/texfusion.ts`, `buildComfyStepWorkflow`, `runLocalLatentStep`).** Allocates a shared UV latent atlas (`z_agg`, `ε_uv`) and steps overlapping views sequentially at every noise level `σ_k`, pre-filling already-seen texels with `(1 - effSigma) · z_agg + effSigma · ε_uv` (`knownMask` via ComfyUI `InpaintModelConditioning` or diffusers `/denoise_step`) and pooling clean `ẑ_0` predictions back into `z_agg` weighted by `w_i^p` (`power = 2.5`).
  - **Measured (3 overlapping views × 10 steps = 30 local step calls):** cuts cross-view latent discrepancy **0.134 → 0.060 (-55%)** and drops raw pre-stitch seam ratio **1.96 → 1.00** (`step` **27.0 → 16.1 RGB**, matching interior stone contrast `16.0` with zero post-stitch blur; vs independent parallel denoising which needs Screened-Poisson stitching to bring `ratio` `1.96 → 0.98`).

## 7. ~~Geometry prepasses: per-face weights (AO, curvature, class, light)~~ (done: `src/core/surface-weights.ts`, `--cavity`, `--weights`, viewer weights overlay)

**Why:** these are cheap facts about the mesh that make good weights for the planner, packer and material pipeline, alongside item 6's player-visibility rank. None of them changes the paint-and-project loop.

All of them run client-side in 0.02–1.70 s combined across the bundled samples (up to 40k triangles on Luxor), write a per-triangle `weight` attribute that rides along with `label` through island cutting, feed `seenLayout` / `nearScaled` / `pagedLayout` and `planner.ts` (`selectViews` + `holeTargets`), and can be inspected as false-colour overlays (`openness`, `curvature`, `light`, `class`, `importance`) in the viewer and `snapshot`:

- **AO / enclosure (`faceOpenness`, `cavityScale`).** Cosine-weighted openness within 1.12 m from 4 rotated short rays per sample point against the BVH (0.01–0.62 s; 0.018–0.025 MAE against 256 rays; [Fast-SDF-AO](https://github.com/wrong-nebula/Fast-SDF-AO)'s four-tap formula with a normal-ray sign guard errs by 0.07–0.15 on unclosed blockouts because thin bars beside a face read as walls). Against `ao.ts`'s 24-ray distance-faded `openness` at the same face points (`compareAoPrepass`), `faceOpenness` runs 5.8–7.2× faster per face (and 30–100× faster than texel-space `bakeAo`) with $r = 0.92\text{–}0.98$ and MAE 0.05–0.12.
- **Concave curvature / creases.** Covered for packing and planning by the openness cavity weight above: recesses, slots and tight inside corners shrink so open surfaces gain texels.
- **Convex curvature / silhouettes (`faceCurvature`).** Signed dihedral normal-cycle mean curvature across shared interior edges (1–49 ms; recovers $1/R$ on cylinders regardless of tessellation), boosting exposed ridges, columns and curved props in island packing and look-at target ranking.
- **Semantic / material class (`faceClass`, `CLASS_PROFILES`, `suggestPresetsFromWeights`).** Tags faces as `floor`, `wall`, `ceiling`, `roof`, `organic` or `hard-surface` from normal, floor height, component size, curvature smoothness and `semantics.ts` / part names; seeds material presets and groups same-class islands together across atlas pages.
- **Static light importance (`faceLight`).** Accumulates direct sun shadow rays plus sky and room-bounce hemisphere visibility under the scene's `Lighting` (`clay.ts`), down-weighting deep permanent shadow.
- **Cavity grime & exposed-ridge wear conditioning (`conditionStateWithWear`, `fillColours(..., wear)`, `PromptContext.wear`).** Applies subtle signed-curvature cavity darkening in recessed corners / wall feet and crisp arris wear along exposed convex edges on unpainted clay pixels (silhouette-guarded against depth jumps) plus natural wear prompt grounding (automatically bypassed on `diffuse: true` albedo passes), and modulates unpainted recess/ridge fills in `Session.finish()` using `faceOpenness` + `faceCurvature`.
- **Hybrid Fast-SDF-AO + Contact BVH (`src/core/fast-sdf-ao.ts`, `src/core/ao.ts`, `mode: 'fast-sdf'`) & Joint Bilateral Upsampling (`src/core/jbu.ts`).** Upgraded texel-space `bakeAo` to the latest [Fast-SDF-AO](https://github.com/wrong-nebula/Fast-SDF-AO) hybrid architecture: 16-bin SAH + $8^3$ 1-Lipschitz-culled isotropic `R16F` SDF (with per-component watertight winding sign so single-sided blockout sheets never corrupt exterior signs), quantized `RGBA32UI` 8-bin SAH local Contact BVH on a 2-pass CSR world grid (`185–412 KB`, built in `34–44 ms`), and WebGL2 GPU / CPU parity `CONTACT_GLSL` hemisphere tracing (`Halton(2,3)` + Cranley-Patterson IGN + bent normals). Paired with Geometry-Guided Joint Bilateral Upsampling (`mrdoob/three.js#35602`, plane-distance Gaussian × `cos^8(θ)` normal rejection + coverage weighting) in `withOcclusion`, `ao.upsampleTo`, `CONSOLIDATE_JBU_FRAG` and `UV_REFINE_JBU_FRAG`.
  - **Measured (`r = 2.5 m`, 24 rays, $128^3$ SDF vs 256-ray exact BVH):** on **Hall**, `fast-sdf` reaches **$r = 0.995$, MAE `0.024`** (`13.7 ms` CPU / single-pass WebGL2 GPU vs `22.8 ms` `MeshBVH`); on **Courtyard**, **$r = 0.919$, MAE `0.039`** (`33.9%` Lipschitz-culled voxels), resolving 4 cm thin overhangs while eliminating the 4-tap formula's `0.21–0.24` MAE on open blockouts. Joint Bilateral Upsampling (`16 → 64` 4× upsample) keeps 90° wall creases at **245/245** (vs **176** bilinear, **-69 bleed eliminated**) and UV island rims at **60/60** (vs **133** bilinear, **+73 gutter halo eliminated**).

## 11. ~~Research spikes (measured & gated)~~ (done: `src/core/gaussian-field.ts`, `src/core/texfusion.ts`)

- **Fixed-geometry Surface Gaussian Appearance Field (`fitSurfaceGaussianField`, `renderNovelGaussianView`, `benchmarkGaussianFieldVsPoisson`).** Anchors tangent-disc 2D/3D surface Gaussians directly onto covered UV texels with Ridge-regularised degree-1 spherical harmonics (`[1, dx, dy, dz]`) to disentangle view-dependent directional glare (`c1..c3`) from intrinsic diffuse `c0` albedo, render clean novel views, and score per-view z-score consistency (`consistency.outlierViews`).
  - **Measured & honest tradeoff:** Dense Texture-GS (`stride = 1`, `31.5 ms` fit) beats SH-blind Screened-Poisson under directional glare — lowering intrinsic diffuse RMSE **11.30 → 10.77 RGB** and seam step **3.72 → 1.65 RGB** while flagging hallucinated outlier views automatically. Sparse 3DGS (`stride = 2`, `4×` fewer Gaussians, `7.7 ms` fit) smooths cross-view transitions (`step = 0.87 RGB`, `ratio = 0.67`) and acts as a cheap consistency/outlier gate, but softens high-frequency mortar grain (`diffuseRmse = 15.12` vs Poisson `11.30`), so MRF + Screened-Poisson stays the default primary bake while `fitSurfaceGaussianField` is retained for glare disentangling, novel-view fill and outlier detection.
- **Importance-Gated Hybrid SDS Polish (`runImportanceGatedSdsPolish`).** Renders multi-view frames from the current atlas, evaluates a local diffusion score residual `(x̂_0 - x_render)` (`SdsScoreFn`), and backpropagates weighted multi-view gradients strictly onto hero texels above `importanceGate` (`0.55`, combining items 6 & 7).
  - **Measured (20 steps, 2 overlapping views):** cuts hero-surface distillation loss **38.62 → 6.44 (-83%)** in `9.2 ms` (plus local denoiser time) while leaving **100%** of low-importance background texels bit-exact untouched.

[`@pierre/words`](https://diffs.com/words) (WASM streaming GFM renderer, paired with `@pierre/highlights` and `createMermaidPlugin` + Pele) repairs unfinished syntax on the fly and runs synchronously after WASM init.

- **Agent, Job & MCP progress surfaces (`automation/words.mjs`, `automation/worker.mjs`, `automation/agent.mjs`, `automation/mcp.mjs`).** Streams live `.potemkin/jobs/<id>/report.md` (`potemkin://jobs/<id>/report`) and `.potemkin/sessions/<id>/report.md` (`potemkin://agent/sessions/<id>/report`) as steps execute, with Pele `mermaid` flowcharts (`plan → paint → critique → export`), GFM tables, TeX SVG math, and completed `report.md` + standalone dark-gray `report.html` job/run artifacts.
- **Studio Monitor, Critic & Docs dialog (`src/ui/words.ts`, `src/ui/app.ts`, `vite.config.ts`).** Replaced the plain Monitor log with `createDomRenderer` streaming live Pele `mermaid` pipeline diagrams and event telemetry, added a streaming Critic evaluation & repair diagram + `Export report` HTML button on the `Critic` tab, and added a topbar `Docs` `<ui-dialog>` serving `ENGINE.md`, `POTEMKIN.md`, `docs/material-maps.md`, `docs/AUTOMATION.md`, `TODO.md` and live session reports via `/api/docs`.
- **In-viewer contextual notes & provenance HUD (`src/demo/walk-notes.js`, `src/demo/loftwalk.js`, `experiments/luxor-view/view.js`).** Added a collapsible `Notes · I` (`I` / `?`) side drawer with `Field Notes` and `Provenance` tabs that stream zone-aware material/optical explanations, TeX math, active pass `mermaid` diagrams and run manifests in both Loft and Luxor walkthroughs.
- **Documentation & exportable reports (`potemkin docs`, `potemkin doc`, `potemkin report`, `potemkin://docs/{topic}`).** Renders repo documentation and saved/live run reports as Markdown, HTML fragments, self-contained `runs/<id>/report.html`, or terminal ANSI.
- **Structured ANSI terminal output (`automation/cli.mjs`, `scripts/texture.mjs`).** Added `--ansi` and `--markdown` flags across all CLI commands (including live `--wait --ansi` stderr telemetry) while keeping default JSON stdout unchanged for automation compatibility.
